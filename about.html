Our story

Built around the people who grow our food.

Salone Harvest is a student website concept exploring how digital tools can connect Sierra Leonean farmers and buyers.

🌿Local roots. Shared growth.

Why Salone Harvest?

Local agriculture deserves a stronger digital connection.

Farmers and buyers may rely on word of mouth, physical visits and informal networks to find one another. This concept brings product discovery and contact information into one accessible place.

The website is designed with a Sierra Leonean audience in mind, using familiar produce examples such as rice, cassava, sweet potato and pepper.

Our mission

Make local produce easier to discover and local connections easier to start.

As a prototype, Salone Harvest demonstrates a possible digital service. Listings and contact details should be verified before any real-world transaction.
